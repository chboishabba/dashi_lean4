This book includes basic material on <b>general topology</b>, introduces
algebraic topology via the <b>fundamental group</b> and covering spaces.

<p>
There were two editions in Russian, in 1988 and 2000. The
<b><font size=+1><a href="http://www.ams.org/bookstore-getitem/item=mbk-54">
first English</a></font></b>
edition was published in 2008. Here is its 
<b><font size=+1><a href="eng-book-nopfs.pdf"> abridged version</a></b> with
proofs and solutions removed.
The <b><font size=+1><a href="rus-book.pdf">third Russian</a></font></b>
edition is to be published in 2009-2010.


<p>The book is written mainly for students with a limited experience
in mathematics, but determined to study the subject actively.
The material is presented in a concise form, proofs are presented
separately from formulations.
Theorems, however, are formulated in detail, and the reader is expected
to treat them as problems. This is done in order to emphasize the role of
formulations and proofs.

<p>More about the book can be found in the
<b><font size=+1><a href="e-intro.html">Introduction</a></font></b>, where
we describe its other specific features and the ideas behind
them. To learn more, take a look on
<b><font size=+1><a href="e-toc.pdf">Table of Contents</a></font></b>.
