> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Supply the theorem-heavy Lean side of the DASHI TOE: typed universal-realization status, concrete C3/representation intertwiners, Monster isotypy/Hom-space continuation, Eisenstein/CM continuation, gauge/QFT algebraic adapters and exact OpenAI theorem routing.

**Tech Stack:** Lean 4 / pinned DASHI mathlib environment, current `Integration/` conventions, RED-first regression owners, OpenAI corpus kept hermetic at its own toolchain pin.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-dashi-toe-universal-realization-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Treat 369/C3 as the candidate universal substrate and actively attempt strong realization bridges.
- Keep conjectural realization distinct from Lean theorem proof; do not weaken the hypothesis merely because its proof is open.
- Reuse current Monster #47/#58 and related source, Eisenstein #69, RH #77, quantum #76, Millennium #53 and OpenAI ingress #78 work rather than cloning it.
- Cross-prover source status is not Agda proof authority.
- Exact OpenAI theorem declarations come from solution modules, never comparator `sorry` targets.
- No kernel-green claim without an observed Lean/Lake run.

## Review Focus

- **Representation transport:** equal character/dimension cannot manufacture actual same-action isotypy.
- **CM transport:** arithmetic `6*zeta*L(chi_-3)` cannot be relabelled as the elliptic L-function.
- **Gauge transport:** U1 bracket collapse cannot manufacture YM→Maxwell action/stress reduction.
- **OpenAI imports:** upstream declaration or metadata cannot be labelled a DASHI port without same-object transport and local checking.
- **Physical interpretation:** Lean algebraic equivalence remains below calibrated physical identity unless the Agda physical realization supplies that attachment.
