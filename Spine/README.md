lanes.  It owns the generic observer, Gram/Schur, ternary phase, trace/norm,
finite-reindexing, and two-regime constructions that should not be restated in
each specialist folder.

The 2026-09-10 intake has two complementary parts:

- the complete TOE, YM, and RH source outputs are indexed in
  `ImportedLeans/aristotle-results/canonical-corpus`, with hashes and original
  archive occurrences retained there;
- `Spine.JMD` promotes the checkable JMD Full Stack Architecture kernels:
  Monster/CRT arithmetic, the prime-valuation FactorVec sheaf, and the finite
  encode--select--prune--diagonal dynamics.

The DOI/QID/Dewey/Wikidata, NS/YM/RH, law, ecology, optics, Moonshine and
other donor lanes are all retained in the provenance corpus.  Their use of the
Spine must be through narrow adapters that state the domain hypotheses and
source status explicitly.  Merely indexing or importing a source does not
establish an empirical claim, a physical theorem, or an Agda kernel receipt.
