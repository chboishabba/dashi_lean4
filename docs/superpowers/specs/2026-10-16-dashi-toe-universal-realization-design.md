Date: 2026-10-16

This is the Lean-side mirror of the canonical architectural design for treating DASHI as one candidate physical theory. Lean is a theorem-intensive sanity/proof surface; the cross-domain physical synthesis remains canonically coordinated with `dashi_agda` `DASHI.Unified` and its Round131/GR-QFT spine.

## Governing interpretation

DASHI is one candidate physical theory, not a federation of analogies. The mature 369/C3 layer is the candidate universal kinematic/refinement/orientation substrate. Monster, exceptional geometry, gauge/QFT/GR, quantum information/mereology, matter/chemistry, CM/arithmetic and continuum dynamics are candidate sector realizations, reductions, phases, observers or effective theories of one universal DASHI object.

Lean and Agda are the consistency, provenance and falsification harness. The architecture should permit aggressive candidate identifications while requiring every identification to expose the exact mathematical, transport, physical or empirical obligations required to retain it.
