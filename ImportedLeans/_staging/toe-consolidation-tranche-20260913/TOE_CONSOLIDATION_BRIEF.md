# TOE consolidation brief — 2026-09-13

Treat the accompanying `dashi_agda` tree as the authoritative broad-integration, provenance, and proof-genealogy map.  Treat the accompanying `dashi_lean4` tree as the active, more compressed theorem architecture.  The task is **not** a file-by-file Agda port and not a new proof-history invention: consolidate the existing, already-linked garden into maximally reusable Lean carriers, exact adapters, and real consumer welds.

## Operating rule

Work Pareto-first.  Inspect the Agda canonical spine, `Everything` and cross-domain rollups, provenance records, proof archaeology, and the current NS/YM/RH construction material before selecting work.  Prefer a change that causes several real consumers to factor through one typed Lean carrier over a lane-local translation.

For every retained lane:

1. Identify the Agda owner, source/provenance, predecessors and replacements, downstream consumers, status, and open payments.
2. Search Lean `Spine`, `Synthesis`, `Cuisine`, `AgdaMirror`, `Integration`, and active libraries for a carrier or theorem through which that exact structure factors.
3. If it exists, make the thinnest precise adapter and attach the real consumer.
4. If it does not exist, compare repeated Agda structure across lanes, introduce the smallest legitimate domain-neutral carrier, then route each genuinely matching lane through it.  Do not leave a lane isolated merely because the abstraction is absent.
5. Generalise or reuse an existing carrier rather than creating parallel definitions.  Prefer one carrier plus typed adapters over many local encodings.

Maximum reuse is legitimate only when the factoring map and its hypotheses can be stated.  Preserve `WrongType`/non-factorability where it cannot.  A missing transport theorem is an explicit next payment, never a reason to flatten the material or assert an analogy.

## Required lineage and status discipline

Every promoted Lean carrier or adapter must record the Agda owner/source module, Lean carrier/theorem, exact consumer, hypotheses, proof/status class, predecessor/successor or supersession relation where applicable, source/DOI/QID/Dewey/provenance metadata, unresolved payments, and explicit not-yet-transportable seams.

Keep separate: proved theorem; conditional compiler/interface; open or false-pinned payment; no-go/counterexample; empirical/source receipt; bookkeeping/provenance; and conjectural/physical promotion.  Never claim compilation, theorem closure, or physical/mathematical closure without a receipt.

Create/maintain a Lean canonical-spine registry, opt-in garden/Everything rollups, and a Lean↔Agda lineage ledger:

`Agda owner → Lean carrier → Lean adapter → Lean consumer → proof/status → provenance`.

The registry must identify canonical ownership, consumers, status, provenance, and the no-parallel-definition policy, without turning the whole garden into one mandatory build target.  Build typed DOI/QID/Dewey/publication/entity/source-status infrastructure, preserving the firewall: identifiers supply identity, classification, and provenance—not mathematical authority.
